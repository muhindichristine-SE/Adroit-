How It Works

📋 Projects

Tap New Site Audit to create a project. Fill in the client, location, contractor, consultant and date. Tap ✎ Edit on the project header to update these details at any time.

📷 Adding Issues

Tap 📷 Add Issue to log a finding. Take a photo or upload one — or tap Skip photo to add a comment-only issue. Speak your note using the mic button or type it. Set severity and who it's assigned to.

✏️ Annotating Photos

On any issue with a photo tap ✏ Annotate. Draw arrows, circles, rectangles or freehand. Pick colours and line thickness. Tap ↩ Undo to step back one stroke at a time. Tap Save Annotations when done.

🔄 Status & Reordering

Tap the coloured status badge on any issue card to cycle Open → In Progress → Closed instantly. Drag the ⠿ handle to reorder issues. Tap ☐ Select for bulk close or delete.

📄 Exporting

Tap 📄 PDF Preview to generate and preview the full report. Tap ⬇ Download in the preview to save it. Tap 📧 Send to open your email app with the report details pre-filled.

💾 Backup & Restore

All data is stored only on this device. Tap Backup regularly to download a JSON file. If you ever clear your browser or switch devices, tap Restore and pick your backup file to recover everything.
